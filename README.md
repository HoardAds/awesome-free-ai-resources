## Limited-Time Offers

*These expire or rotate — check them first.*

- [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/pricing) - 25+ free model variants (`:free`) behind one API key: 20 requests/minute, up to 50 requests/day, no card required.
- [Jina AI](https://jina.ai) - Every new API key comes with a one-time grant of 10,000,000 free tokens across Reader, Embeddings, Reranker and Classifier.
- [AWS Free Tier](https://aws.amazon.com/free/) - New accounts get $100 in credits at sign-up plus up to $100 more for completing onboarding activities, spendable across AWS on the 6-month Free plan.
- [Secret](https://www.joinsecret.com) - A free account unlocks 300+ negotiated SaaS deals and freebies for startups, out of a 600+ deal vault.

→ Details, limits and eligibility: [hoardads.com/free-resources/#limited-time](https://hoardads.com/free-resources/#limited-time)

## Free Tiers

*Free plans that stay free — the working backbone of a $0 stack.*

- [PostHog](https://posthog.com/pricing) - Monthly free allowances that reset every month: 1M analytics events, 5,000 session recordings, 1M feature-flag requests, 100K exceptions — no card.
- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/) - $0 forever plan metered at 300 credits/month covering deploys, compute, bandwidth and requests, with custom domain and free SSL.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/pricing) - Hobby plan: 100 GB data transfer, 1M function invocations and 1M edge requests free (personal, non-commercial use).
- [Cloudflare Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) - 100,000 requests/day, free KV and D1 quotas, and unlimited free static-asset hosting.
- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/pricing) - 500 MB Postgres database, 50,000 monthly active auth users, 1 GB file storage and 500K edge-function invocations/month.
- [Convex](https://www.convex.dev/pricing) - 1M function calls/month, 0.5 GB database storage and 1 GB file storage for teams of up to 6 — with no inactivity pausing.
- [Clerk](https://clerk.com/pricing) - Drop-in authentication free up to 50,000 monthly retained users per application, with unlimited applications.
- [LaunchDarkly](https://launchdarkly.com/pricing) - $0 Developer plan: unlimited seats and feature flags, 5 service connections, 1,000 client-side MAU/month.
- [Liveblocks](https://liveblocks.com/pricing) - Collaboration infrastructure (presence, comments, notifications) free for up to 100 monthly active users.
- [MongoDB Atlas](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing) - M0 cluster free forever: 512 MB storage on shared RAM/vCPU — enough for learning, prototypes and tiny apps.
- [Apify](https://apify.com/pricing) - $5 of platform usage credit every month for web-scraping and automation actors; unused credit does not roll over.
- [Databricks Free Edition](https://www.databricks.com/learn/free-edition) - A permanent free workspace with serverless compute, notebooks and SQL for learning data and AI; daily quotas apply.
- [Cloady](https://cloady.com/docs/hosting) - Free cloud workspace: 0.5 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 5 GB SSD and 10 GB/month transfer (EU1/USE1/USW1 regions — read the limits before you build on it).
- [Sandscale](https://sandscale.dev/pricing) - Free plan for Firecracker microVM sandboxes used by AI coding agents: 100 awake vCPU-hours a month, 3 boxes at once, 5 GB storage and one workspace.

→ Details, limits and eligibility: [hoardads.com/free-resources/#free-tiers](https://hoardads.com/free-resources/#free-tiers)

## Startup Credits

*Application-based programs. The headline numbers are real — and so is the fine print, which the Center spells out for each one.*

- [Google for Startups Cloud Program](https://cloud.google.com/startup) - Up to $2,000 in credits (Start), up to $200,000 over 2 years (Scale), and up to $350,000 for AI-first startups.
- [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/activate/) - $1,000 in self-serve Founders credits; up to $100,000 when applying through an Activate Provider (accelerator or VC).
- [Microsoft for Startups](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups) - Azure credits on a milestone ladder: $200 at sign-up, rising through $5,000 and $25,000 to $150,000 as you verify and build.
- [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/) - Up to $250,000 in platform credits, in tiers of $5,000 / $25,000 / $100,000 / $250,000, for product startups up to Series B.
- [MongoDB for Startups](https://www.mongodb.com/startups) - Atlas credits valid for 12 months — up to $5,000, with up to $5,000 more for AI startups — plus program support tiers.
- [PostHog for Startups](https://posthog.com/startups) - $50,000 in PostHog credits for 12 months, for companies under 2 years old with less than $5M raised.
- [OVHcloud Startup Program](https://startup.ovhcloud.com) - €10,000 in cloud vouchers at Start level and up to €100,000 at Scale level — direct application, no VC referral needed.
- [Notion for Startups](https://www.notion.com/startups) - The Business plan free for up to 6 months via a startup partner (3 months direct), including the plan's Notion AI credit allowance.

→ Details, limits and eligibility: [hoardads.com/free-resources/#startup-credits](https://hoardads.com/free-resources/#startup-credits)

## Genuinely Free

*No card, no trial clock, no catch worth the name.*

- [Kaggle Notebooks](https://www.kaggle.com/code) - Free NVIDIA T4 x2 GPU notebooks under a weekly quota of ~30 GPU hours, plus a TPU pool; a verified account unlocks the accelerators.
- [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com) - Free browser notebooks with GPU access (a T4 when one is available); quotas are unpublished and best-effort by design.
- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/) - A Photoshop-class editor in the browser: every editing tool is free, PSD/AI/Sketch files open natively, and files never leave your device.
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/) - The complete professional 3D suite under the GPL: free for any purpose, including commercial work, and what you create is yours.
- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) - Hand-drawn-style whiteboard, free forever with no sign-up; open source under MIT, so you can self-host it too.

→ Details, limits and eligibility: [hoardads.com/free-resources/#truly-free](https://hoardads.com/free-resources/#truly-free)

## Why trust this list

Every entry was checked by hand against the provider's official pricing or program page — not a blog post, not another list. The last full verification pass was **11 November 2026**; limited-time offers are re-checked monthly and the rest quarterly. If an offer's terms change materially, the entry is updated or pulled.

## Contributing

Additions are welcome if they are genuinely free: a real free tier, a credit program, or a free-forever tool. Please link the provider's **official** page and state the concrete free allowance (numbers, not adjectives) in the description. Not accepted: affiliate links, plain discounts dressed up as freebies, time-limited trials, and anything you have not verified on the official page yourself.

Spotted a stale entry? Open an issue — corrections keep this list worth starring.
